## Stable Boundary Map

RunEngine step evidence recording is an atomic event-chain command: `EvidenceSubmitted` is followed by causally linked `EvidenceAccepted`, optional `EvidenceLinkedToTarget`, and `CoverageRecomputed`. The evidence ledger still projects accepted evidence entries as the stable read model, while audit and coverage can prove the submitted evidence was linked to targets before coverage was recomputed.

RunEngine verdict issuing is atomic: `VerdictRequested` precedes `VerdictIssued`, then `VerdictAllowedClosure` or `VerdictBlockedClosure`. Residual risk adds `ResidualRiskAccepted`; blocked gaps add `NextGapSelected`. Task verdict projections still derive from `VerdictIssued`; closure audit facts do not replace successful `RunClosed` causation.

RunEngine step claiming records `StepPlanned` before `StepClaimed`, followed by the causally linked `StepInstructionIssued` that freezes the executable StepInstruction. Current-step and surface projections still use `StepInstructionIssued`; `StepPlanned` and `StepClaimed` are audit facts around planning and runner ownership.

RunEngine step submission records `StrategyAdvanced` after causally linked `StepCommitted`; it mirrors the latest committed step id/iteration/status as an audit fact, while the next executable step still comes from replay plus the current strategy definition.
